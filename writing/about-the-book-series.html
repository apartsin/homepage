About the Hands-On AI Science book series: what these volumes are for, and how a company of AI agents, under heavy human supervision, writes each one to be complete, accurate, and genuinely enjoyable to read.

Each volume aims to give a complete treatment of its subject, combining the theoretical concepts with the modern, practical tools a working practitioner actually uses. They are written by a company of AI agents under heavy human supervision and steering, with a human setting the direction, making the judgment calls, and approving every chapter.

The books are not meant to be read cover to cover. Each is a focused, self-contained reference: a place students in the matching course can look things up, a resource instructors can build on when preparing a course, and a compact but complete treatment of a specific subject for practitioners who need one.

The agents work together so the book stays enjoyable and readable for humans: many worked examples, step-by-step explanations, and dense cross-references that tie the whole subject together. Each agent below owns one narrow concern, from deep explanations and code pedagogy to fact integrity, pacing, and final publication checks.

  • CompleteTheory and modern practical tools, treated end to end.
  • Human-steeredAI agents draft and review; a human directs and approves.
  • ReadableWorked examples, step-by-step explanations, cross-references.
  • CheckedDedicated agents for facts, figures, code, and consistency.
