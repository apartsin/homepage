Grant Sanderson's 3Blue1Brown series on deep learning is the best visual introduction I know to how neural networks, transformers, and LLMs actually work. Watch these in order before you fight with the code; the intuition will save you hours later.

The Deep Learning series, in order

Each video stands on its own, but they build on each other from the ground up.

  • Intro

    Large Language Models explained briefly

    A lightweight, high-level intro to what LLMs are and what they do. Good starting point that sets the stage for everything below.

    Watch on 3Blue1Brown ↗

  • Chapter 1

    But what is a Neural Network?

    An overview of what a neural network is, introduced in the context of recognizing hand-written digits. The one to send someone who has never seen a network before.

    Watch on 3Blue1Brown ↗

  • Chapter 2

    Gradient descent, how neural networks learn

    An overview of gradient descent in the context of neural networks; a method used widely throughout machine learning to optimize how a computer performs on a task.

    Watch on 3Blue1Brown ↗

  • Chapter 3

    What is backpropagation really doing?

    An overview of backpropagation, the algorithm behind how neural networks learn. Builds real intuition for what the gradient signal is telling each weight to do.

    Watch on 3Blue1Brown ↗

  • Chapter 4

    Backpropagation calculus

    The math behind backpropagation: chain rule, partial derivatives, and how the error propagates back through the network. Watch if you want the formalism behind the picture from chapter 3.

    Watch on 3Blue1Brown ↗

  • Follow-up

    Analyzing our neural network

    A closer look at what the trained network from the earlier chapters actually learned; what the neurons represent and where the pretty story starts to break.

    Watch on 3Blue1Brown ↗

  • Chapter 5

    Transformers, the tech behind LLMs

    A visual introduction to transformers. Focuses on the overall structure and word embeddings, the pieces you need before attention makes sense.

    Watch on 3Blue1Brown ↗

  • Chapter 6

    Attention in transformers, step-by-step

    Demystifies attention, the key mechanism inside transformers and LLMs. This is the video to rewatch until Q, K, V click.

    Watch on 3Blue1Brown ↗

  • Chapter 7

    How might LLMs store facts?

    Unpacks the multilayer perceptrons inside a transformer and how they may store facts. Where "the model knows things" starts to become a mechanical picture.

    Watch on 3Blue1Brown ↗

How to use these: watch the video that matches whatever you are struggling with in the course, then read the equivalent chapter in the course notes or a textbook. Video builds the intuition, text nails down the details.
