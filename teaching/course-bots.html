Home / Teaching / Course Syllabi / Course Bots

I built these AI-powered advisors to help students get started faster by refining project ideas, narrowing scope, and structuring strong proposals for the Hands-On AI Science courses.

LLM Course 5 bots

Bots for preparing and validating your project submission in the LLM course, from the proposal through the midterm to the final presentation and repository.

Student Project Advisor Bot

Helps students brainstorm, scope, and structure project proposals for the Building Language AI course. Covers topic selection around LLMs, conversational agents, retrieval-augmented generation, and tool-use pipelines.

Open Bot

Proposal Reviewer Bot

Reviews your project proposal against the course requirements, the motivating use case, problem statement, novelty, planned models and data, and evaluation plan, and tells you what to strengthen before you start building. Pairs with the Project Proposal guide.

Open Bot

Midterm Report Reviewer Bot

Reviews your midterm report and presentation, the refined definition, previous-work review, dataset and exploratory analysis, baseline, and initial results, and flags gaps against the Midterm Presentation guide.

Open Bot

Final Presentation Reviewer Bot

Reviews your final presentation for a clear technical argument, the achievements, methodology, experimental results, interpretation, and conclusions, against the Final Presentation guide.

Open Bot

Repository Submission Validator Bot

Validates your final GitHub submission, the structure, README, reproducibility, and aligned code, data, and slides, against the GitHub Submission guide before you hand it in.
