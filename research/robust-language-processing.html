Robust Language Processing

Home / Research / Robust Language Processing

Making language technology reliable when the input is noisy, informal, degraded, or ambiguous, the way real text and speech actually arrive. This work runs from three decades of industry systems (search, handwriting, speech, document understanding) to today's large language models (LLMs), across healthcare, public safety, talent management, IT, education, and more, spanning peer-reviewed papers, patents, industry projects, and supervised student research.
