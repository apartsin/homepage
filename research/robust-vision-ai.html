Robust Vision AI

Home / Research / Robust Vision AI

Keeping visual perception reliable when the picture itself is working against you, the degraded, low-light, blurred, occluded, and geometrically extreme conditions real cameras produce. Running from three decades of industry systems to today's models, this work spans public safety and defense, biomedical imaging, automotive and mobility, image enhancement, streaming and media, and model optimization, holding recognition, detection, and reconstruction accurate under motion, haze, sensor noise, and heavy occlusion, across peer-reviewed papers, preprints, patents, industry projects, and supervised student research.
